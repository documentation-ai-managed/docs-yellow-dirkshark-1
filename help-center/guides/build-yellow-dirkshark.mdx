## Build Tutorial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Model Page">
    Go to the Yellow DirkShark MOC page on Rebrickable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Required Parts">
    Click on the "Parts" tab for an inventory of needed LEGO bricks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Instructions">
    Find and download PDF instructions linked on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather Pieces">
    Sort the required parts from your own LEGO collection. Mark off what you have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Building">
    Follow each instruction step in the guide to assemble your Yellow DirkShark!
  </Step>
</Steps>
